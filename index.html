Frandy Jose Proch Angeles

Frandy Jose Proch Angeles, known online as FrandyJory, is a Dominican-born trader, entrepreneur, former music creator, and investor. Throughout his life he has explored music, basketball, e-commerce, investing, and financial trading while working toward his goal of achieving financial freedom.

Early Life

Frandy was born on February 4, 2006, in Santiago, Dominican Republic. At the age of seven, he moved to Fort Lauderdale, Florida, where he continued his education and adapted to life in a new country.

His elementary school years were challenging. There were frequent student conflicts and difficult situations that shaped his outlook on discipline and personal growth.

After elementary school, he attended Sunrise Middle School. During those years, he spent much of his time focused on his own interests and future ambitions. Although he had a small group of friends, he often preferred working on personal goals and hobbies.

Basketball Journey

At fifteen years old, Frandy developed a strong passion for basketball and dreamed of joining a competitive basketball team.

To improve his skills, he enrolled in the All-American Basketball Camps. His first experiences were difficult, and he quickly realized how much work would be required to reach a high level.

Despite practicing almost every day, the results did not develop as he had hoped. Over time, he chose not to pursue basketball as a career. However, basketball remained a major part of his life and became one of his favorite ways to relax, stay active, and clear his mind.

Music Career

At fourteen years old, Frandy began creating music and experimenting with digital content.

His most successful project was a song called "Goofy Ahh Beat", which he says reached approximately 1.3 million listeners within six months.

The song's popularity encouraged him to continue making music and exploring a creative career. Although the project generated income, he eventually decided that music was not the long-term path he wanted to pursue.

Entrepreneurship

After stepping away from music, Frandy explored online business and e-commerce through Shopify.

He experimented with products, advertising, and marketing strategies. Although the business efforts did not produce the results he wanted, the experience helped him better understand entrepreneurship, sales, and persistence.

Trading Career

At sixteen years old, Frandy discovered financial trading and became interested in understanding how markets operate.

In the beginning, he practiced using paper trading accounts and spent time following trading signals from other people. Eventually he realized that becoming successful would require building his own understanding of the markets.

From that point forward, he focused on studying market structure, fair value gaps, risk management, trading psychology, and multi-timeframe analysis.

Today he continues working toward becoming a consistently profitable trader, with a primary focus on the Nasdaq 100 and stock index markets.

Personal Life

Outside of trading, Frandy enjoys basketball, technology, investing, entrepreneurship, and learning new skills.

His experiences in sports, music, business, and trading have all played a role in shaping his mindset and approach toward growth and self-improvement.
